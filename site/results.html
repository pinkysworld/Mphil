Temporal Behaviour

What changes once time is taken seriously

Walk-forward evaluation

The 19-window rolling evaluation produced a mean macro-F1 of 0.7908, with monthly performance ranging from 0.6690 to 0.9289. The variation is large enough to matter and suggests that a single split does not tell the full story.

Invariance and discriminability

API tokens and artefact tokens are highly discriminative, with within-training macro-F1 values of 0.9744 and 0.9641. The shift measures are view-specific, so the study does not combine them into one cross-view invariance ranking.

Leakage Control

Current ablation outcome

Random split

Controlled and permitted runs both returned macro-F1 0.8792 in the current implementation, giving a delta of 0.0000.

Chronological split

Controlled and permitted runs both returned macro-F1 0.7830, again producing a delta of 0.0000 in the present ablation pass.

This null result is limited to audited exact family-name segments in the implemented allow-listed inputs. Indirect proxies and fields outside that representation remain possible leakage routes.

Interpretation

H4a is supported for ECE. H4b is not supported: Brier score, log loss, and selective-routing transfer are worse after calibration in this sensitivity experiment.

Model fitting, calibration fitting, threshold selection, and future testing use four strictly ordered date segments. The result is one sensitivity analysis on the archived corpus, not repeated-window or external validation.

Decision-support methodology

An executable four-state contract, not a simulated SOC

Contract test

All eight deterministic cases pass and exercise States A-D under explicit confidence, temporal, scope, and evidence conditions.

Retrospective replays

The confidence lane maps 8,840 cases to State B and 956 to State C; seven of 19 temporal windows map to State C; seven of nine held-out-family experiments map to State D.

Boundary

No empirical replay reaches State A, and no analyst outcome is measured. The demonstrator validates policy execution, not production readiness or usability.
